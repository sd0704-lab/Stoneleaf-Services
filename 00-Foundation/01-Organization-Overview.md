# Stoneleaf Services — Organization Overview

**No Stone Left Unturned**

## Overview

Stoneleaf Services is a developing independent technology, cybersecurity, investigation, intelligence collection, and analysis organization.

The organization is being developed progressively, beginning with a strong foundation in information technology infrastructure, networking, systems administration, cybersecurity, troubleshooting, and technical documentation.

As technical knowledge, professional experience, certifications, business capabilities, and supporting infrastructure mature, Stoneleaf Services is intended to expand into more advanced cybersecurity, digital investigation, intelligence collection, research, and analytical services.

The long-term objective is to develop Stoneleaf Services into an independently operated professional services organization capable of providing technical, investigative, and analytical services within the owner's demonstrated competencies and applicable legal and professional requirements.

---

## Mission

The mission of Stoneleaf Services is to apply systematic investigation, technical expertise, critical thinking, and evidence-based analysis to complex technical and informational problems.

The organization will emphasize thoroughness, accuracy, documentation, continuous learning, and systematic problem solving.

The Stoneleaf Services motto reflects this philosophy:

> **No Stone Left Unturned**

## Organizational Development Strategy

Stoneleaf Services will be developed incrementally rather than attempting to provide advanced services before the necessary capabilities have been established.

Development will progress through several interconnected capability areas:

1. **Information Technology**
2. **Networking and Systems Administration**
3. **Cybersecurity**
4. **Digital Investigations and Incident Response**
5. **Research and Information Collection**
6. **Open-Source Intelligence**
7. **Intelligence Analysis**
8. **Professional Technical and Analytical Services**

Each capability will be supported by appropriate education, technical training, certifications, practical exercises, documented projects, and hands-on experience.

Services will only be added to the organization as the technical knowledge, practical competency, business processes, and any applicable legal or professional requirements are established.

## The Stoneleaf Services Home Lab

The Stoneleaf Services home lab serves as the primary technical development, testing, and training environment for the organization.

The lab is designed to simulate components of a small organizational IT environment where infrastructure can be designed, deployed, administered, secured, monitored, intentionally disrupted, investigated, repaired, and documented.

The initial on-premises environment consists of:

| Hostname | Platform | Primary Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SLS-FW01` | pfSense | Firewall, routing, DHCP, NAT |
| `SLS-DC01` | Windows Server | Active Directory Domain Services and DNS |
| `SLS-LNX01` | Ubuntu Server 24.04 LTS | Linux server administration and services |
| `SLS-WS01` | Windows 11 | Investigations workstation |
| `SLS-WS02` | Windows 11 | Intelligence and analysis workstation |
| `SLS-WS03` | Windows 11 | Management and administrative workstation |

VMware provides the initial virtualization platform.

Future development is expected to incorporate Microsoft Azure and Microsoft Entra ID to create a hybrid on-premises and cloud environment.

## Troubleshooting Philosophy

Systematic troubleshooting is a core capability of Stoneleaf Services.

Problems should be approached through structured analysis rather than assumption or uncontrolled configuration changes.

The general troubleshooting methodology will emphasize:

1. Identify and clearly define the problem.
2. Gather relevant information and evidence.
3. Establish the scope of the problem.
4. Identify possible causes.
5. Develop and prioritize hypotheses.
6. Test hypotheses systematically.
7. Identify the root cause.
8. Develop and implement corrective action.
9. Verify full functionality.
10. Document findings, actions, and lessons learned.

This methodology will be applied across networking, operating systems, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, digital investigations, and other technical problems.

## Professional Principles

Stoneleaf Services will be developed around the following principles:

### Thoroughness

Technical and analytical problems should be investigated completely rather than resolved through unsupported assumptions.

### Evidence

Conclusions should be supported by available technical data, documentation, reliable sources, or other relevant evidence.

### Accuracy

Information should be verified when practical, and uncertainty should be identified rather than concealed.

### Documentation

Technical decisions, investigations, changes, and findings should be documented clearly enough to be reviewed and reproduced.

### Security

Systems, information, credentials, and sensitive data should be protected throughout their lifecycle.

### Legality and Ethics

Technical, investigative, and information-collection activities must remain within applicable laws, authorization, contractual boundaries, and professional standards.

### Continuous Improvement

Lessons learned from projects, incidents, troubleshooting exercises, education, and experience should be incorporated into future Stoneleaf Services practices.
